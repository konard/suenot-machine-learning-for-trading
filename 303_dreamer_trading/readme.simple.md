# Dreamer Trading - Explained Simply

## What is it?

Imagine practicing a sport entirely in your dreams before the real game. You dream about kicking a soccer ball hundreds of times - trying different angles, different speeds, different techniques. When you wake up and play for real, you already know what works!

That's exactly what **Dreamer** does for trading. It builds a "dream world" of the stock market inside a computer. Then it practices buying and selling millions of times in this dream world. When it finally trades with real money, it already knows good strategies!

## How does it work?

Think of it in three steps:

### Step 1: Learn How the World Works
First, Dreamer watches real market data - like prices going up and down. It learns the patterns, just like how you learn that a ball falls when you drop it.

### Step 2: Dream About Trading
Then it starts "dreaming." It imagines what might happen tomorrow, next week, or next month. It tries buying in its dreams and sees what happens. It tries selling and sees what happens. It dreams thousands of different scenarios!

### Step 3: Pick the Best Strategy
After all that dreaming, it picks the strategies that made the most dream-money. Now it's ready for the real market!

## Why is this cool?

- **No risk while learning**: Just like you can't get hurt practicing in your dreams, Dreamer can't lose real money while it's learning.
- **Super fast learning**: In real life, you can only trade once per moment. In dreams, you can practice millions of times in seconds!
- **Ready for surprises**: Because it dreamed up so many different scenarios, it's prepared for unexpected market moves.

## A Simple Example

Imagine you want to learn to trade lemonade:
1. You watch how lemonade prices change with weather, seasons, and events
2. You dream up 1000 different summer days and practice setting prices
3. You find out: "When it's hot AND there's a parade, charge more!"
4. Now when summer comes, you already know the best strategy!

## The Magic Part

The really special thing about Dreamer is that its dream world gets better and better. As it sees more real data, its dreams become more realistic. It's like having a crystal ball that gets clearer the more you use it!
