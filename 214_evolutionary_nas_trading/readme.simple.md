# Evolutionary NAS Trading - Simple Explanation

Imagine a nature show where animals evolve over generations. The strongest survive and have babies that are even stronger. Evolutionary NAS works the same way — neural networks "breed" and "mutate" over generations until the fittest architecture survives!

## What is this about?

Think of it like a talent show for robots. You start with a bunch of robots, each built a little differently. Some are tall, some are short, some have big brains, some have small ones. You give them all the same task: "Try to predict if a stock price will go up or down."

Some robots do great, and some do terribly. The ones that do well get to be "parents" and make new baby robots. The baby robots inherit traits from their parents — maybe one parent is good at spotting patterns and the other is fast at making decisions. The baby gets a mix of both!

Sometimes a baby robot gets a random change — a "mutation." Maybe it gets an extra brain module or loses one it did not need. Most mutations do not help, but once in a while, a mutation makes a robot way better at its job.

## How does it work?

1. **Start**: Create a bunch of random neural networks (our "robots").
2. **Test**: Let each one try to predict stock prices.
3. **Pick winners**: The best ones become parents.
4. **Make babies**: Combine parts from two parent networks to make new ones.
5. **Mutate**: Randomly change a few things in the baby networks.
6. **Repeat**: Do this over and over for many generations.

After many generations, you end up with neural networks that are really good at the job — even though nobody designed them by hand!

## The cool part: Aging

There is a neat trick called "aging evolution." Even if a robot is really good, if it is too old, it has to retire. This makes room for young robots with fresh ideas. It is like how in nature, even the strongest animals eventually make way for the next generation.

## Why is this useful for trading?

The stock market is always changing. A strategy that works today might not work tomorrow. By constantly evolving new neural networks, we can keep up with the market's changes — just like how animals evolve to survive in changing environments!
