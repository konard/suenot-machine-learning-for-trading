# Personalized FL: The "Shared Textbook and Personal Experience" Analogy

Imagine you are learning how to trade in the markets.

### 1. The Shared Textbook (Global Model)
You read a classic textbook on trading. It describes the basics: what a trend is, how indicators work, and how inflation relates to currency rates. This textbook is useful to every trader in the world.

### 2. Personal Experience (Local Adaptation)
However, this textbook doesn't know that you only trade rare cryptocurrencies at 3:00 AM. To be successful in your specific niche, you need to take the knowledge from the textbook and "tweak" it based on your own observations.

### 3. Personalized FL
In our system:
1. **The Federation** writes the "World's Best Textbook" by combining the experience of all banks.
2. **You** take this textbook, but you don't replace your brain with it. You use it as a foundation, layering your secret tricks on top of it.

**The Result**: You have the collective power of the community's knowledge, but your strategy remains perfectly tuned to your unique trading style.
