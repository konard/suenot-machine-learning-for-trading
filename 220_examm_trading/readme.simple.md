# EXAMM Trading - Explained Simply!

## What is EXAMM?

Imagine evolving a diary system. Some diaries remember everything (LSTM), some only recent stuff (GRU). EXAMM creates brand new diary designs by mixing and evolving them — finding the perfect memory system for tracking stock prices!

## How does it work?

Think about it like this: you have a class of students, and each student has their own special way of taking notes about what happened to a stock price.

- **Student A** writes down EVERYTHING — every single price change, forever. That is like an LSTM.
- **Student B** only writes down the recent stuff and erases old notes. That is like a GRU.
- **Student C** keeps just the bare minimum. That is like an MGU.

Now, here is the cool part: **EXAMM is like a teacher who runs a competition**.

The teacher says: "Everyone, try to predict tomorrow's price using your notes!" The students who make the best predictions get to teach their note-taking style to new students. But with a twist — sometimes the new students **mix** two styles together, or **change** something small about their style.

Over many rounds of this competition, the note-taking styles get better and better. Eventually, you end up with amazing note-taking systems that nobody could have designed by hand!

## The Island Trick

EXAMM puts groups of students on different "islands" (like different classrooms). Each island develops its own style. Once in a while, the best student from one island visits another island and shares their technique. This way, good ideas spread everywhere, but each island still keeps its own flavor.

## Why is this useful for trading?

Stock prices are tricky. Sometimes they go up steadily (a trend), sometimes they bounce around randomly, and sometimes they crash suddenly. No single diary style works perfectly for all these situations.

By evolving many different diary styles and testing them on real price data, EXAMM can find the perfect memory system that knows:
- When to pay attention to old information
- When to forget old stuff and focus on what is new
- When to sound the alarm because something unusual is happening

## The Big Idea

Instead of a human choosing "I will use LSTM" or "I will use GRU," we let the computer evolve its own memory system from scratch. It is like how nature created eyes, ears, and brains — not by design, but by millions of tiny improvements over time. EXAMM does the same thing, but for neural network memory, and it only takes minutes instead of millions of years!
